About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science, located at Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu, providing facilities for both students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct their practical sessions and research.

Eventually, the Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) in Serdang was transferred to Kuala Terengganu, and the branch campus was renamed Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) in June 1996. On 1 February 2007, it was officially given full university status and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.

Today, UMT is recognized as Malaysia's premier marine science and aquatic resource university, focusing heavily on oceanography, marine biology, maritime studies, and sustainable environmental management.
